Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

At PIP Energy, your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or engage our solar engineering services, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA) and applicable regulations.

1. Who We Are

PIP Energy ("PIP Energy", "we", "us", or "our") is a solar engineering company providing residential, commercial, and critical-infrastructure solar solutions across Nigeria. For any privacy-related questions, contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

  • Contact details — such as your name, email address, phone number, and installation address when you request a consultation or quote.
  • Project information — details about your property, energy usage, and appliances you share with us (including values entered into our Load Calculator).
  • Communications — messages you send us by email, WhatsApp, or social media.

Information we collect automatically

  • Usage data — such as pages visited, time on site, and referring links, collected to help us improve the website.
  • Device data — such as browser type, device type, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

Our Load Calculator processes the figures you enter within your browser to estimate your energy needs. These values are not stored on our servers unless you choose to share them with us to request a quote.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide consultations and quotes.
  • To design, install, and support your solar energy system.
  • To communicate with you about your project, appointments, and service updates.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data where it is necessary to perform a contract or take steps at your request before entering one, where we have your consent, where we have a legitimate business interest that is not overridden by your rights, or where processing is required to comply with a legal obligation.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business (for example, installers, logistics partners, and IT or hosting providers), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and legal counsel where necessary.
  • Authorities where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site features.

7. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

9. Your Rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website links to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Instagram, and X (Twitter). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external services, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
